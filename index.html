What is PaedsENGAGE?

PaedsENGAGE is a pilot programme led by KK Women’s and Children’s Hospital (KKH) and National University Hospital (NUH) that partners trained GP clinics to manage mild to moderate childhood conditions in the community, while helping children who need emergency care get referred appropriately.

PaedsENGAGE clinic finder — frequently asked questions

What is PaedsENGAGE?

PaedsENGAGE is a pilot programme led by KK Women’s and Children’s Hospital (KKH) and National University Hospital (NUH) that partners trained GP clinics to manage mild to moderate childhood conditions in the community, while helping children who need emergency care get referred appropriately.

How do I find a PaedsENGAGE clinic near me in Singapore?

Search by area, clinic name, address, or doctor, then filter by location, day, opening hours, or “Open now”. Matching clinics appear on the map with a direct Google Maps link for each clinic.

Where does the clinic information come from?
